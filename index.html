01 — Featured build

TowGo vehicle recovery, coordinated.

A full platform for Greater Accra that takes a driver from “I'm stuck” to a settled payout. It verifies towing cover, sends each job to one truck only, records photo evidence and handover codes, keeps custody records for authorities, and settles money through a double-entry ledger.

Two Flutter apps (vehicle owners and tow drivers), six Laravel portal areas for dispatchers, operators, insurers, fleets, officers and storage yards — all on one modular Flask API backed by PostgreSQL and PostGIS.
